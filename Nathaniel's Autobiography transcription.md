## Page 25 & 26: Investing in the USA: Vehicle Overview

Building wealth requires understanding the primary investment vehicles available in the US market, balancing risk, return, and liquidity:

1. **Traditional Savings Accounts:** Yields ~4% APY. FDIC insured up to $250,000. Extremely safe and liquid, but returns are too slow for real wealth accumulation ($100k generates only $4k/yr).
2. **Government Bonds / Bank CDs:** Yields ~5% APY. Highly secure choice if willing to lock up capital for fixed periods, trading liquidity for modest guaranteed yield.
3. **Mutual Funds:** An excellent balance between CDs and individual stocks. Offers higher growth potential through diversification and automated management for $25k–$100k portfolios.
4. **Individual Stocks:** High return potential coupled with significant downside risk depending on business fundamentals and market volatility.
5. **Cryptocurrency:** Unlike stocks, crypto lacks underlying corporate revenue backing. However, Bitcoin functions as a global borderless asset class.
6. **Property Rentals:** Single-handedly one of the strongest investments possible. Real estate offers leverage, equity growth, and cash flow—even with a mortgage.
7. **Property Flipping:** Purchasing distressed homes, renovating them, and selling at a premium. Extremely lucrative, but requires substantial manual labor, material management, and capital outlay.
8. **IRAs & 401(k)s:** Pre-tax retirement vehicles that compound tax-deferred growth. Essential for long-term security, backed by decent APYs and federal protections.
