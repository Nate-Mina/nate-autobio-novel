# Concept: The System & The Soul

## Genre
Literary Fiction / Biographical Fiction

## Target Audience
Adult (25+) — readers who appreciate introspective narratives, engineering philosophy, and raw psychological honesty.

## Theme
The tension between emotional chaos and logical order — how the analytical mind processes trauma, love, addiction, and healing. The novel explores whether human experience can be engineered or whether some spaces remain forever beyond the reach of mathematics.

## Premise
Nathaniel Mina — mechanical engineer, entrepreneur, and recovering addict — has spent his life processing every human emotion through the lens of systems engineering. From calculating the pharmacokinetics of his own opioid addiction to constructing game-theuristic utility models to repair romantic trust, Nate's mind is both his greatest asset and his most intimate enemy. This is the story of how a man who tried to debug his own heart finally learned that some equations have no solution.

## Main Characters
- **Nathaniel "Nate" Mina** — Protagonist. RIT-educated mechanical engineer. Gemini. Processes everything through systems logic. Recovering opioid addict. CEO of Pure Computers.
- **Karam Mina** — Nate's father. Born in Cairo, Egypt. Laid off from Kodak. Traditional, silent, homeschooler. Broke both ankles in a swing-set accident.
- **Kathie Eberhardt** — Nate's mother. Born in NY. Became county worker after Karam taught her computers. Engaged in custody battle and 22-day abduction.
- **Jack** — Nate's best friend at RIT. Betrayed Nate by sleeping with his ex-girlfriend while lying face-to-face.
- **Kelly** — Early girlfriend. Bipolar, sheltered, from Palmyra NY. Cheated with her boss.
- **Rachael** — Girlfriend met NYE 2011. Secretly married another man while dating Nate.
- **Nicole** — Alcoholic girlfriend. Nate helped her get clean but recognized the pattern.
- **Sabrina** — The love of Nate's life. Trauma survivor. The hospital misunderstanding. The trust equation.
- **The Beast** — Nate's personification of his addictive voice (right brain).
- **The True Self** — Nate's personification of his rational mind (left brain / prefrontal cortex).

## Setting
Primarily Rochester, New York — Henrietta, Highland Hospital, RIT campus, the basements and Burger Kings of a collapsing childhood. Also: Freeport Bahamas (cruise ship deployments), Palmyra NY, and the abstract landscape of Nate's internal dual-brain warfare.

## Tone
Introspective, raw, engineering-literate. First-person retrospective. Unflinching. The voice of a man who rebuilt himself from scratch — not once, but continuously.

## Structure
- **Prolog:** The Equation (present-day framing)
- **Chapters 1-11:** Chronological life story
- **Epilog:** Synthetic Reflection

## Key Motifs
- Engineering metaphors applied to human experience
- The Trust Equation: T = (R + I) / S
- The Left Brain / Right Brain duality (True Self vs. Beast)
- Bioavailability and optimization
- The Bucket List Paradox
- Pessimistic Bias and trauma firewalls
- AVRT (Addictive Voice Recognition Training)

## Word Count Target
~7,500 words per chapter (adult profile), ~82,500 total
